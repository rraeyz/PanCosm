# photometry/sa98-landolt-lco

- **What:** Landolt field SA 98, LCO 1 m (elp, fa16), 2024-12-12, B (`-0096`) and V (`-0101`), proposal CLN2024B-007.
- **Source and licence:** Las Cumbres Observatory (LCO) science archive, public frames (archive-api.lco.global). Acknowledge: "This work makes use of observations from the Las Cumbres Observatory global telescope network."
- **Get the whole set:** LCO archive, OBJECT=SA98, 2024-12-12.
- **Used for:** Transformation coefficients against Landolt (2009) standards.
- **Size of the files used:** 76 MB, 2 files.
