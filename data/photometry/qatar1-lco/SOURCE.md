# photometry/qatar1-lco

- **What:** Qatar-1 b transit night, LCO 0.4 m (elp, sq41), 2026-11-02/03, proposal LCOEPO2020B-004. 24 of the night's 265 frames (the first 24, `-0105` to `-0128`), fpack-compressed, already calibrated by the LCO pipeline (e91).
- **Source and licence:** Las Cumbres Observatory (LCO) science archive, public frames (archive-api.lco.global). Acknowledge: "This work makes use of observations from the Las Cumbres Observatory global telescope network."
- **Get the whole set:** Full night: query `https://archive-api.lco.global/frames/?OBJECT=Qatar-1&start=2026-11-02&end=2026-11-04&public=true`. Target (0-based x, y) ≈ (1338.0, 1197.3) on the first frame; four comparison stars used in the checks: (1255.7, 1347.1), (832.8, 1873.4), (699.9, 1761.4), (1284.8, 1441.5).
- **Used for:** Light curves, the aperture scan, the plate solution (Gaia), speed. Full-night results are in `plans/photometry.md`.
- **Size here:** 327 MB, 24 entries.
