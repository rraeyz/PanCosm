# photometry/ai-dra-tess

- **What:** TESS full-frame-image cut-out of AI Dra (eclipsing binary), sector 24, 21 × 21 pixels, 1226 cadences of 30 min.
- **Source and licence:** NASA TESS mission data through MAST TESScut (public domain; cite TESScut, Brasseur et al. 2019).
- **Get the whole set:** `https://mast.stsci.edu/tesscut/api/v0.1/astrocut?ra=254.0758&dec=52.6983&y=21&x=21&sector=24` (follow the redirect, `curl -L`).
- **Used for:** Target-pixel-file photometry, minima, O−C (P = 1.19880 d).
- **Size of the files used:** 11 MB, 1 files.
