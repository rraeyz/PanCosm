# spectroscopy/feros-ai-phe

- **What:** FEROS reduced spectra of the eclipsing binary AI Phe and of tau Cet, programme 091.D-0414(B); `list.csv` lists the epochs.
- **Source and licence:** ESO Science Archive Facility, public data. Acknowledge: "Based on data obtained from the ESO Science Archive Facility" with the programme ID.
- **Get the whole set:** ESO archive, programme 091.D-0414(B). Published comparison: Gallenne et al. 2019, Maxted et al. 2020 (papers not stored here).
- **Used for:** SB2 radial velocities, disentangling, orbit.
- **Size here:** 43 MB, 12 entries.
