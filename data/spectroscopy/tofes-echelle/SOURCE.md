# spectroscopy/tofes-echelle

- **What:** TOFES fibre echelle (Tartu Observatory), R 32 000, 46 orders, test night 2024-06-21: bias, flats, darks, ThAr (`*_Calibration_*`), the Sun (sky), Arcturus, Vega. A subset of the 200 files.
- **Source and licence:** Zenodo record 17968312, CC-BY 4.0 (Tartu Observatory).
- **Get the whole set:** Whole night (720 MB): `https://zenodo.org/records/17968312`, `TOFES_testobservations_2024-06-21.tar.gz`.
- **Used for:** Echelle orders, 2-D wavelength solution, radial velocities (Arcturus), spectral types.
- **Size of the files used:** 154 MB, 22 files.
