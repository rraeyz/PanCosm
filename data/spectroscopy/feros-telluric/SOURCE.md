# spectroscopy/feros-telluric

- **What:** FEROS (MPG/ESO 2.2 m) reduced spectra of tau Cet (with a telluric-corrected copy) and zeta Phe, programme 091.D-0414(B).
- **Source and licence:** ESO Science Archive Facility, public data. Acknowledge: "Based on data obtained from the ESO Science Archive Facility" with the programme ID.
- **Get the whole set:** ESO archive, FEROS phase-3 products of programme 091.D-0414(B).
- **Used for:** Telluric correction.
- **Size of the files used:** 11 MB, 3 files.

## Files used in the checks

Download these into this folder (git ignores the data files here; only notes and small text files are kept).

- `taucet.fits` (3.1 MB)
- `taucet_tell.fits` (4.6 MB)
- `zetaphe.fits` (3.1 MB)
