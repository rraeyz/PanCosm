# spectroscopy/efosc-longslit

- **What:** ESO NTT EFOSC2 long-slit raw frames, 2018-12-31 / 2019-01-01 (LZW-compressed `.Z`, needs `uncompresspy` or `gzip -d`), with `night.csv`.
- **Source and licence:** ESO Science Archive Facility, public data. Acknowledge: "Based on data obtained from the ESO Science Archive Facility" with the programme ID.
- **Get the whole set:** ESO archive, EFOSC raw frames of those nights.
- **Used for:** Long-slit reduction: apall, He-Ar arc identification.
- **Size of the files used:** 8 MB, 9 files.
