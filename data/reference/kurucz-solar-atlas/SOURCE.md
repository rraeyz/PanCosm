# reference/kurucz-solar-atlas

- **What:** Kurucz (2005) solar flux atlas, air wavelengths in nm, normalised flux, telluric-free.
- **Source and licence:** kurucz.harvard.edu, free for research use; cite Kurucz 2005, Mem. S.A.It. Suppl. 8, 189.
- **Get the whole set:** `http://kurucz.harvard.edu/sun/fluxatlas2005/fluxspliced.2005`.
- **Used for:** Solar template for wavelengths and radial velocities.
- **Size of the files used:** 30 MB, 1 files.
